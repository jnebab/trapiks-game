# M17: city performance on the real map

Goal: the whole-city sandbox on the real Metro Manila map meets plan §3.9. With 20k active vehicles, the median step is at most 12 ms native and at most 25 ms in wasm. Mean A* is at most 0.5 ms, and bounded route work is at most 4 ms per step.

## Starting point

This is a provisional orchestrator run after M16's map landed. Another heavy job was running at the same time, so the numbers are noisy. The command was `bench web/public/maps/metro-manila.bin.gz --vph 80000 --warmup 9000 --ticks 1000`.

| Measure | Result | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Active vehicles | 18.7k mean | 20k |
| Step median / p95 / max | 10.7 / 22.2 / 85.7 ms | median ≤ 12 ms |
| Mean route | 4.93 ms (11,222 expansions) | ≤ 0.5 ms |
| Route work per step | 11.0 ms | ≤ 4 ms |

On the synthetic 120×120 city, per-vehicle passes dominated (`docs/perf.md`). On the real map, routing dominates: each route expands 11k links, against about 1k on the synthetic grid.

## 1. Measure first

1. Add `--vph` values to the bench until the mean active count is 20k ± 1k on the real map. Record the vph. Use that vph for every number in this milestone.
2. Report the phase split (mean ms per step) on the real map, as `docs/perf.md` does for synthetic.
3. **Route diagnostics.** Add an example or a bench flag that reports all of these for the same 2,000 seeded city trips:
   - mean expansions and µs per route with free-flow costs, and with the costs after warm-up;
   - weight 1.0 and weight 2.0;
   - Euclid only, and with ALT.

   Also report the mean ratio of ALT bound to true cost at the start link. This shows whether the landmarks or the congestion are the problem.

Commit nothing from this step. Put the numbers in the report, because they decide which fixes in §2 are worth it.

## 2. Routing

Apply these in order. Stop when mean A* is ≤ 0.5 ms and route work is ≤ 4 ms per step, and report each step's effect.

1. **Landmark quality.**
   - Check that all 8 landmarks lie in the largest strongly connected component and on the map's periphery.
   - Farthest-point selection by free-flow time can bunch landmarks along the expressways. If it does, choose them by the planar method instead: split the plane around the map centre into 8 equal angular sectors, and in each sector take the active link farthest from the centre that lies in the largest SCC. Ties go to the lower id.
   - The landmark count stays 8, because wasm memory holds 2 × 8 × f32 per link, about 16 MB on the real map.
2. **Congestion-aware bound.** ALT bounds are computed on free-flow times, so they fall well below the true cost in congestion.
   - Keep a global congestion factor `c`: the length-weighted mean of `free_time / ema_time` over active links, recomputed with the EMA refresh every 600 ticks and clamped to [0.25, 1].
   - Use `h = weight × bound / c`. It is deterministic because it is computed in link id order.
   - This trades optimality for speed, like the existing weight. Report the mean route-length excess against Dijkstra on the 2,000 trips, which must stay at or below 15 %.
3. **Search hygiene.** Profile `Router::route_into`:
   - Clearing per-route state must be O(touched), not O(links).
   - The heap must not hold stale duplicates without bound.
   - No allocation per route.

   Fix whatever the profile shows.

Changes that alter routes change `state_hash`. That is allowed in this milestone. Update the recorded golden constants, and make sure the native = wasm hash check still passes.

## 3. Per-vehicle passes

Do these only if the step median is still over 12 ms after §2. Each one must keep `state_hash` bit-identical before and after (a pure optimization):

1. Compute each vehicle's leader and gap once per step. Share them across the acceleration, decision and lane-change passes. Invalidate a vehicle's entry only when an earlier pass in the same step moves it to another lane or link.
2. Evaluate discretionary (MOBIL) lane changes only for vehicles within 150 m of their next decision zone or behind a leader slower than 0.8 × their desired speed. Mandatory changes stay unconditional. This one changes the hash, so record the new constants.

## Acceptance

- Native city step median is ≤ 12 ms at 20k ± 1k active vehicles on the real map. Mean A* is ≤ 0.5 ms, and route work is ≤ 4 ms per step.
- Wasm city step median is ≤ 25 ms, or the gap is reported with the phase split if headless software rendering makes that unmeasurable.
- Mean route excess is ≤ 15 % against Dijkstra.
- `bash scripts/check.sh` passes, and so do the real-map tests (`TRAPIKS_REAL_MAP=1`).
- `docs/perf.md` is rewritten by the orchestrator with the real-map numbers from the implementer's report.
- No comments. Do not modify `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/` or `.claude/`. Do not stage files.
