# M14: vehicle kinds and lane changes

Goal: Manila traffic looks and behaves like Manila traffic.
- **Vehicle kinds:** jeepneys and buses join cars. Each kind has its own length, acceleration, desired speed and sprite.
- **Mandatory lane changes:** vehicles get into the lane range of their next movement.
- **Discretionary lane changes (MOBIL):** vehicles overtake slower ones, with a keep-right bias.

All changes stay deterministic, and M6's invariants still hold. The **predecessor rule** matters most: a committed vehicle never sits behind an uncommitted one on a lane.

## Vehicle kinds (`src/vehicle/kind.rs`)

```rust
pub enum VehicleKind { Car, Jeepney, Bus }
```

| Kind | Length | `a` (m/s²) | `v0` factor | Spawn share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Car | 4.4 m | 1.2 | 1.00 | 80 % |
| Jeepney | 6.5 m | 1.0 | 0.90 | 15 % |
| Bus | 12.0 m | 0.8 | 0.85 | 5 % |

- **Drawing the kind:** it is drawn once, at trip creation, right after the destination is accepted: `rng.below(100)`, with `< 80` giving Car, `< 95` Jeepney, and otherwise Bus.
  - It is stored in `Trip.kind` and carried in `RoutedTrip`, and it is hashed with the queued trips.
  - A `Blocked` spawn retries with the same kind.
- **Spawning:** `LinkQueues`' spawn closure calls `spawn_kind(route, kind)`. `spawn(route)` keeps its signature and uses Car. The colour draw at spawn is unchanged.
- **Store:** `VehicleStore` gains `kind: Vec<VehicleKind>`, which is hashed. `length(slot)` is `kind.length()`.

### Per-kind kinematics

- `idm::acceleration(v, v0, gap, dv, a)` and `idm::interaction` take the vehicle's `a`, including in the `2√(a·b)` braking term. `IDM_MAX_ACCEL` stays only as the Car value.
- A vehicle's desired speed is `link speed × factor`. It is used by:
  - `desired_speed`
  - the spawn `entry_speed`
  - `time_to` (M6), for both the vehicle itself and the other vehicle `B`, each with its own `a` and `v0`
- **M7b stats:**
  - A trip's `free_flow` time uses `v_ref × factor`.
  - `accrued_delay` uses `1 − v / (v_ref × factor)`.
  - So a bus cruising at its own desired speed accrues no delay.

### Lengths (replacing `CAR_LENGTH`)

**The rule:**
- A gap to the vehicle ahead subtracts **that vehicle's** length.
- Space that a vehicle is about to occupy uses **its own** length.

| Place | New form |
|---|---|
| `leader.rs` gaps (same place and lookahead) | `leader_s − s − length(leader)` |
| Spawn gap (`spawn.rs`) | `leader_s − length(leader) − span_start ≥ IDM_MIN_GAP`. The spawner's front is at `span_start`, and its body hangs behind it |
| Spawn block for a vehicle about to land (M7a) | blocked when `distance < IDM_MIN_GAP + length(spawned)` |
| `has_space` (`space.rs`) | `free = last.s − length(last) − span_start`, or the span length when the lane is empty. `required = length(self) + IDM_MIN_GAP + Σ (length_i + IDM_MIN_GAP)` over the counted vehicles. The "empty lane, n = 0" escape stays. The occupants' lengths come from a new `Occupancy` accessor over the sorted and pending entries of a key, which replaces reaching into `pending` |
| `cluster_exit_has_room` | `free ≥ length(self) + IDM_MIN_GAP`, with the same `free` |

**Conflict clearing:**
- `CONFLICT_CLEAR_MARGIN = 2.6` becomes a pure margin, with no body in it.
- `CONFLICT_STOP_BACK = 7.0` is a new constant for the obstacle stop distance. For cars, every value below equals M7a's.

| Rule | New form |
|---|---|
| Rule 4 limit (`entry.rs`) | `c.s_other + length(B) + CONFLICT_CLEAR_MARGIN` |
| Rule 5 `t_self` distance | `span_end − s + c.s_self + length(self) + CONFLICT_CLEAR_MARGIN` |
| Conflict obstacle stop point (`sim/conflict.rs`) | `c.s_self − CONFLICT_STOP_BACK` |
| Conflict obstacle window | `[c.s_other − CONFLICT_STOP_BACK, c.s_other + length(other) + CONFLICT_STOP_BACK]` |

**Tests and invariants:**
- `check_invariants`:
  - The spacing within every place is at least the leader's length.
  - The conflict body window is `[s − length − 1.6, s + 1.6]` per vehicle.
- `junction_priority.rs` uses the vehicle's own length.
- `junction_space.rs` uses its own length plus `IDM_MIN_GAP`.
- `vehicles_corridor.rs` uses the leader's length.
- `CAR_LENGTH` is deleted. `grep -rn CAR_LENGTH` must find nothing.

### Snapshot style

- `style = kind as u8 * 16 + color`.
- **Decode:** the frame is `style >> 4`. The tint is `VEHICLE_COLORS[style & 15]` for Car and `0xFFFFFF` otherwise.

## Lane changes (`src/vehicle/lane_change.rs`)

```rust
pub const LC_SAFE_DECEL: f64 = 4.0;
pub const LC_POLITENESS: f64 = 0.3;
pub const LC_THRESHOLD: f64 = 0.1;
pub const LC_KEEP_RIGHT_BIAS: f64 = 0.3;
pub const LC_COOLDOWN_TICKS: u64 = 30;
pub const LC_ANIMATION_TICKS: u64 = 30;
pub const LC_MIN_DISTANCE_TO_END: f64 = 60.0;
pub const LC_DISCRETIONARY_EVERY: u64 = 5;
```

### Store additions (all hashed)

- **`lane_from`:** `lane_from == lane` means no animation.
- **`lane_change_tick`:** set to the spawn tick at spawn, so a new vehicle never changes lanes in its first step.
- **Every place rewrite sets `lane_from = lane`:**
  - link entry (`reset_junction_state`)
  - reroute
  - the M10a lane clamp
  - the M10b flyover moves
  - spawn
- `Sim::lane_change_count() -> u64` counts applied changes.

### Allowed lanes

- The `from_lanes` of the vehicle's next movement.
- Every lane on the last link of its route.

### Eligibility

A vehicle is eligible when:
- it is link-placed, not committed, and not pending (`occupancy.index_of(slot)` is `Some`)
- its link has at least 2 lanes

**Mandatory:** the current lane is outside the allowed range.
- The only candidate is the adjacent lane toward the range.
- It needs `tick − lane_change_tick ≥ LC_ANIMATION_TICKS`, with no other cooldown and no `LC_MIN_DISTANCE_TO_END`.
- It is taken whenever it is safe, regardless of incentive.

**Discretionary:** the current lane is inside the allowed range.
- The candidates are the adjacent lanes that are also inside the range.
- Each of these must hold:
  - `tick − lane_change_tick ≥ LC_COOLDOWN_TICKS`
  - `span_end − s ≥ LC_MIN_DISTANCE_TO_END`
  - `(tick + slot) % LC_DISCRETIONARY_EVERY == 0`, which keeps MOBIL's cost bounded and is deterministic

### Neighbours on the target lane

- **New leader:** the vehicle that `leader()` would return if the vehicle were on the target lane at the same `s`.
- **Order on a link:** B is ahead of A when `B.s > A.s`, or when `B.s == A.s` and `B.slot < A.slot`. This is the occupancy order (`occupancy.rs`). Every "ahead", "behind" and "nearest" in this spec uses it.
- **New follower:** the link-placed vehicle directly behind the vehicle on the target lane. The lookup covers the sorted entries and the pending ones, as `last_on` does. A pending entry on the target lane, spawned this tick at `span_start`, is a follower with gap `s − length(self) − span_start`.
- **Landing vehicles:** when the target lane has no link-placed vehicle behind the vehicle, sorted or pending, look at every movement with `to_link == link` and `to_lane == target`. In each, take the vehicle with the highest `s`, which lands next. Its gap is `(s − length(self) − span_start) + (movement.length − its s)`. The one with the smallest gap is the new follower.

### Safety (every change, mandatory ones included)

1. The gap to the new leader is ≥ 0, and the gap from the new follower is ≥ 0, with lengths per the rule above.
2. `a_self_new ≥ −LC_SAFE_DECEL`.
3. `a_follower_new ≥ −LC_SAFE_DECEL`.
4. A link-placed new follower must not be committed.
   - This keeps the predecessor rule: everything behind an uncommitted vehicle on a lane is uncommitted, so the rule holds after every change.
   - A landing follower is checked only by 1 and 3.

### Incentive (discretionary only, MOBIL with IDM accelerations)

- `(a_self_new − a_self_old) + p·((a_newfollower_new − a_newfollower_old) + (a_oldfollower_new − a_oldfollower_old)) > LC_THRESHOLD + bias`.
- The bias is `+LC_KEEP_RIGHT_BIAS` for changes to the left (a higher lane index) and `−LC_KEEP_RIGHT_BIAS` for changes to the right. Right is lane 0.
- So a left change needs more than 0.4, and a right change needs more than −0.2. A vehicle in free flow returns right.

### Step placement

The lane-change pass runs right before M6's decisions pass, so decisions see the final lanes.

1. **Candidates:** a read-only pass over the eligible vehicles, in slot order, collects `(slot, target_lane)`. A mandatory move comes first. Otherwise the higher incentive wins, and a tie goes to the right.
2. **Apply,** in slot order:
   1. Re-check safety against the current occupancy, which already includes this tick's earlier changes.
   2. If it is still safe, set `lane_from = lane`, `lane = target` and `lane_change_tick = tick`.
   3. Re-sort that link's contiguous occupancy block (all of its lane keys, since `link_key` orders by link and then lane), and rewrite `index` for the block.
   - A full occupancy rebuild is not allowed, because it costs two 20k-entry sorts per tick on the city.

### Vehicles in the wrong lane at the line

- **Exclusion:** a vehicle is *excluded* while all of these hold:
  - it is uncommitted
  - its lane is outside its next movement's `from_lanes`
  - it is in its decision zone, checked first
  - `arrival_tick != u64::MAX && tick − arrival_tick < WAIT_TIMEOUT_TICKS`, which never underflows
- An excluded vehicle is not a decision candidate and does not commit.
- When the exclusion lapses without a lane change, set `wait_ticks = WAIT_TIMEOUT_TICKS as u32` in the zone-bookkeeping pass before decisions, so the same tick's decision sees the zero courtesy gap. It then decides as today, taking the movement from its current lane (`from_lane` = its lane), without a second full wait.
- **Rule 5:**
  - `candidate()` (`entry.rs`) scans every lane of `m2.from_link`, not only `m2.from_lanes`. Same-link movements never conflict, so a vehicle entering from an unusual lane is otherwise invisible to crossing traffic.
  - Rule 5's skip list gains excluded vehicles, so crossing traffic never yields to a vehicle that cannot commit.

### Several lanes feeding one movement

A movement is one occupancy queue with one path, but a Through movement can now be entered from several lanes. Entries are therefore serialized:

- **Leader:** a committed link-placed vehicle's `leader()` also considers the nearest committed vehicle ahead, by the link order above, on any other lane of the same link that is heading to the same movement. The smaller of the two gaps wins.
- **`has_space`:** the counted vehicles are the movement's occupants plus every committed vehicle on any lane of the link heading to the same target, whatever its position. This is conservative: a committed vehicle behind still follows through the leader rule. The walk covers the link's contiguous occupancy block front to back, with M6's early exit as soon as `required` exceeds `free`.

### Lane choice on entry

- **`landing_lane`** gains `from_lane`. The landing lane is `clamp(from_lane, next movement's from_lanes)`, capped at the target's lane count. At the end of the route, today's rule is unchanged.
  - So an overtaker in lane 1 stays in lane 1 through degree-2 nodes.
  - `has_space` passes the approach lane.
- **Spawn lane:** the allowed lane with the largest `free` (as in `has_space`), with ties going to the lowest index.

### Pose

- The lateral offset interpolates from `lane_from` to `lane` with smoothstep over `LC_ANIMATION_TICKS` since `lane_change_tick`.
- A link-to-movement transition during the animation jumps to the movement path. This is accepted.

## Rendering (`render/vehicle-atlas.ts`)

**Atlas:** one texture holding 3 frames at 10 px/m, `resolution: 4`:
- **Car:** the M9c sprite, white, so it takes the tint.
- **Jeepney:** 6.5 × 2.1 m.
  - a silver `#D9D9D9` body
  - a red `#E5484D` and yellow `#F2C94C` side stripe
  - a dark windshield
  - a darker roof-rack line
- **Bus:** 12 × 2.5 m.
  - blue `#4A7BD0`
  - a dark windshield
  - a row of side windows, drawn as thin dark bars

**Pools:** one `ParticleContainer` pool per kind (three containers), all sharing the atlas source.
- Pool slots change vehicles every frame (M8), and a per-particle texture change is not uploaded with static `uvs`. A pool per kind avoids that.
- Cars keep `dynamicProperties.color`.

**Particles:**
- `anchorX: 1.0` and `anchorY: 0.5`, so the pose point, the vehicle's front at `s`, is the sprite's front edge.
- Scale stays 0.1. Each frame's pixel size encodes its length.

**Tint:** cars use the palette tint. Jeepneys and buses use `0xFFFFFF`.

## Tests

1. **`kinds_spawn_shares`:** 10,000 trip draws land within ±1.5 percentage points of 80/15/5.
2. **`bus_following`:** on `four_way(1, 200.0)`, a single lane, with route `[0, 5]` for both vehicles:
   - A car spawned while a bus sits near `span_start` waits until `bus.s − 12 − span_start ≥ IDM_MIN_GAP`.
   - The car then follows the bus through the junction, keeping `gap ≥ 0` using the bus length.
3. **`mandatory_change`:** on `four_way(3, 300.0)`, with route `[0, 3]` in link ids: in from the north, out to the east, which is a left turn.
   - First assert that its `from_lanes` excludes lane 0.
   - Spawn the vehicle, then put it in lane 0 with `place_vehicle_for_test(slot, lane)`. This is a `fixtures`-feature `Sim` method that rewrites `place` and `lane_from` and rebuilds the occupancy.
   - It reaches the allowed range before its decision zone, and invariants hold.
4. **`mobil_overtakes`:** on `corridor()` (Primary 2/2):
   - Spawn a bus at tick 0 and a car at tick 30, both routed over all 3 roads. Put the car in lane 0 with `place_vehicle_for_test`.
   - Within 600 ticks the car changes to lane 1, passes the bus, and later returns to lane 0.
   - At the degree-2 nodes both vehicles commit, and the serialization rule holds the car behind the bus while both are committed. So the pass may only complete on the third road. This is expected: do not change the rule to make the pass happen sooner.
5. **`wrong_lane_fallback`:** on `four_way(3, 300.0)`, with a `fixtures`-only `set_lane_changes_for_test(false)`:
   - A vehicle in lane 0 whose next movement is the left turn does not commit before `arrival_tick + WAIT_TIMEOUT_TICKS`, and it completes the turn from lane 0 within 1,500 ticks. The signal's red phase and the south stream may hold it longer than the timeout.
   - Before the exclusion lapses, the south stream does not yield to it. After it commits, the south stream sees it through rule 5, with no conflict overlap, and invariants hold.
6. **`multi_lane_entry`:** on `four_way(2, 200.0)`, two cars spawned in the same tick, side by side in lanes 0 and 1 at the same `s`, both heading straight through. This exercises the tie in the link order.
   - Both commit and enter the movement one after the other, the lower slot first.
   - The spacing is at least the leader's length, and invariants hold.
7. **`lane_change_safety`:** `grid_city(20×20 @ 150)` at 12,000 vph for 3,000 ticks. `check_invariants` holds every tick, and `lane_change_count() ≥ 50`.
8. **Determinism:** `FOUR_WAY_HASH_5000` and `GRID_CITY_HASH_1000` change. Update them, and keep the M8 native = wasm check green.
9. **vitest:** the style decode for each kind and colour.
10. **Bench:** run `bench` on `120x120@150`. Report the step-time change from M7b's numbers. It must stay ≤ 12 ms median native.
