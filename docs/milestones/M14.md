# M14: vehicle kinds and lane changes

Goal: Manila traffic looks and behaves like Manila traffic.
- **Vehicle kinds:** jeepneys and buses join cars. Each kind has its own length, acceleration, desired speed and sprite.
- **Mandatory lane changes:** vehicles get into the lane range of their next movement.
- **Discretionary lane changes (MOBIL):** vehicles overtake slower ones, with a keep-right bias.

All changes stay deterministic, and M6's invariants still hold.

## Vehicle kinds (`src/vehicle/kind.rs`)

```rust
pub enum VehicleKind { Car, Jeepney, Bus }
```

| Kind | Length | `a` | `v0` factor | Spawn share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Car | 4.4 m | 1.2 | 1.00 | 80 % |
| Jeepney | 6.5 m | 1.0 | 0.90 | 15 % |
| Bus | 12.0 m | 0.8 | 0.85 | 5 % |

- `v0 = link speed × factor`.
- **Kind choice:** `rng.below(100)` at spawn, with `< 80` giving Car, `< 95` Jeepney, and otherwise Bus. It is drawn before the colour.
- **Explicit kinds:** `spawn` keeps its signature and always uses Car. `spawn_kind(route, kind)` exists for tests and scenarios. Demand spawns use the drawn kind.
- **Store:** `VehicleStore` gains `kind: Vec<VehicleKind>`, and a vehicle's length is `kind.length()`.
- **Replace `CAR_LENGTH`:**
  - Every gap uses the leader's length: `leader.s − self.s − leader_length`.
  - Every space check uses the checking vehicle's own length plus `IDM_MIN_GAP`: `has_space`, the spawn entry gap, and `CONFLICT_CLEAR_MARGIN` plus length for clearing.
  - The invariant checker uses the leader's length.
- **Snapshot style:** `style = kind as u8 * 16 + color`.
- **Constants:** `FOUR_WAY_HASH_5000` and `GRID_CITY_HASH_1000` change. Update them, and keep the wasm check green.

## Lane changes (`src/vehicle/lane_change.rs`)

```rust
pub const LC_SAFE_DECEL: f64 = 4.0;
pub const LC_POLITENESS: f64 = 0.3;
pub const LC_THRESHOLD: f64 = 0.2;
pub const LC_KEEP_RIGHT_BIAS: f64 = 0.2;
pub const LC_COOLDOWN_TICKS: u64 = 30;
pub const LC_ANIMATION_TICKS: u64 = 30;
pub const LC_MIN_DISTANCE_TO_END: f64 = 60.0;
```

**Eligibility:** a link-placed vehicle that is not committed, on a link with at least 2 lanes, at least `LC_COOLDOWN_TICKS` since its last change, and with `span_end − s ≥ LC_MIN_DISTANCE_TO_END`.

- **Allowed lanes:** `from_lanes` of the vehicle's next movement, or every lane on the last link of its route.
- **Mandatory:** when the current lane is outside the allowed range, the only candidate is the adjacent lane toward the range. It is taken whenever it is safe, regardless of incentive, and ignores `LC_MIN_DISTANCE_TO_END`.
- **Discretionary:** only when the current lane is inside the allowed range. Candidates are the adjacent lanes that are also inside the range. MOBIL with IDM accelerations:
  - **Safety:** the new follower's acceleration after the change is `≥ −LC_SAFE_DECEL`, and the gaps to the new leader and new follower are at least 0.
  - **Incentive:** `(a_self_new − a_self_old) + p·((a_newfollower_new − a_newfollower_old) + (a_oldfollower_new − a_oldfollower_old)) > LC_THRESHOLD + bias`.
  - **Bias:** `+LC_KEEP_RIGHT_BIAS` for changes to the left (a higher lane index), `−LC_KEEP_RIGHT_BIAS` for changes to the right. Right is lane 0.

**Step placement:** after the decisions pass, before accelerations.
1. **Candidates:** a read-only pass over the eligible vehicles in slot order collects `(slot, target_lane)`, preferring the mandatory move, then the higher incentive, with ties going to the right.
2. **Apply** in slot order. Each change re-checks safety against the current occupancy plus the changes already applied this tick on the same link, kept in a small `Vec<(link, lane, s, length)>`. Only then is it applied.
3. **Apply means:** set `lane = target`, record `lane_from = old`, `lane_change_tick = tick`, and update the occupancy entry, re-sorting only that link's range, or rebuild the occupancy when the step made any change.

**Rendering:** the pose interpolates the lateral offset from `lane_from` to `lane` with smoothstep over `LC_ANIMATION_TICKS`.

**Store additions, all hashed:** `lane_from`, `lane_change_tick` and `last_change_tick`.

## Rendering (`render/vehicle-atlas.ts`)

- One atlas texture holding 3 frames at 10 px/m, `resolution: 4`:
  - **Car:** the M9c sprite, white, so it takes the tint.
  - **Jeepney:** 6.5 × 2.1 m. A silver `#D9D9D9` body, a red `#E5484D` and yellow `#F2C94C` side stripe, a dark windshield and a darker roof rack line.
  - **Bus:** 12 × 2.5 m. Blue `#4A7BD0`, a dark windshield and a row of side windows as thin dark bars.
- `Texture` frames share one source, so the `ParticleContainer` can use them.
- **Tint:** cars use the palette tint. Jeepneys and buses use `0xFFFFFF`.
- Particle scale stays 0.1. Each frame's pixel size encodes its length.

## Tests

1. **`kinds_spawn_shares`:** 10,000 draws land within ±1.5 percentage points of 80/15/5.
2. **`bus_following`:** a car behind a bus keeps `gap ≥ 0` using the bus length, and the spawn gap respects the bus length.
3. **`mandatory_change`:** on a 3-lane approach, a vehicle placed in lane 0 by a fixtures-only test hook (`place_vehicle_for_test`), with a next movement that is a left turn needing lane 2, reaches lane 2 before its decision zone. Invariants hold.
4. **`mobil_overtakes`:** on `corridor_lanes(2)`, a bus at `v0 × 0.85` ahead of a car on lane 0. Within 600 ticks the car changes to lane 1 and passes the bus, and later returns right through the keep-right bias.
5. **`lane_change_safety`:** `grid_city(20×20 @ 150)` at 12,000 vph for 3,000 ticks. `check_invariants` holds every tick, and at least 50 lane changes happen.
6. **Determinism:** the updated constants are identical native and wasm (M8 check).
7. **Bench:** run `bench` on `120x120@150`. Report the step-time change from M7b's numbers, which must stay ≤ 12 ms median native.
