# M15b: new connector roads and the build-road tool

Goal: the player can draw short connector roads.
- A road can be straight or curved, and at ground level or elevated.
- Each end snaps to a node or to a point on an existing road. A point on a road splits that road.
- A preview shows the road and its cost before the player commits.

This implements the `AddRoad` row of plan §3.6. It reuses M10b's append, cut, re-point and truncate machinery.

## `EditCommand::AddRoad`

```rust
pub enum Endpoint { Node { node: u32 }, OnRoad { road: u32, at_m: f64 } }
AddRoad { from: Endpoint, to: Endpoint, via: Option<[f64; 2]>,
          lanes_forward: u8, lanes_backward: u8, layer: i8 }
```

- **`at_m`** is the arc length from the road's first point, which is its `from` end.
- **`via`** is the control point of a quadratic Bézier. `None` gives a straight road.
- **Resolved positions:** a `Node` endpoint resolves to the node's position. An `OnRoad` endpoint resolves to the point at `at_m`.
- **Geometry:**
  - Straight: the two resolved positions.
  - Curved: the quadratic Bézier from the `from` position through `via` to the `to` position, sampled at `ADD_ROAD_SEGMENTS = 16`, which gives 17 points.
  - The first and last points are exactly the endpoint positions.
- **Class and speed, from `max(lanes_forward, lanes_backward)`:**

  | Lanes | Class | Speed |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | `Residential` | 40 km/h |
  | 2 | `Tertiary` | 50 km/h |
  | 3–4 | `Primary` | 60 km/h |

- The name is 0, and `roundabout` is false.

### Validation (new `EditError` variants)

Checks run in M10a's order:
1. **Bounds:** nodes and roads exist (`NodeNotFound`, `RoadNotFound`), and roads are not deleted (`RoadDeleted`).
2. **Region mode:** both resolved positions are inside the region circle, and each `OnRoad` road is active in the region (`OutsideRegion`).
3. **Semantics:**
   - `lanes_forward + lanes_backward ≥ 1`, each `≤ 4` (`InvalidLanes`).
   - `0 ≤ layer ≤ 2` (`InvalidLayer`).
   - A `Node` endpoint has at least one active incident road (`EndpointIsolated`). Its roads may have any layer, so a layer-1 road may land on a ground node as a ramp, as OSM bridges do.
   - `at_m` is finite and `10 ≤ at_m ≤ length − 10` (`TooCloseToEnd`). The client snaps to the node instead.
   - The two endpoints must not be the same node, and must not both be `OnRoad` on the same road (`SameEndpoint`). This check runs before the length check.
   - `via` is finite and within 1,000 m of the chord midpoint. The geometry length is at least 10 m and at most 2,000 m (`InvalidLength`).
   - **Angle:** at each end, the new road's direction leaving that endpoint is at least `ADD_ROAD_MIN_ANGLE = 25°` from the direction of every active road leaving it. For an `OnRoad` endpoint, those are the road's two directions at the cut (`AngleTooSharp`).
   - **Crossing:** the new road's polyline must not intersect the polyline of any non-deleted road with the same layer, found with `roads_in_rect` over its bounding box. Intersections within 2 m of either end of the new road are ignored (`CrossesRoad`). Roads on other layers may cross, which makes a bridge or an underpass.
4. **Budget.**

### Cost

`round((200 + 2 × length_m × (lanes_forward + lanes_backward)) × (1 + min(layer, 1))) + 200` per `OnRoad` endpoint.

For example, a 100 m 1+1 ground road between two nodes costs 600, and the same road elevated costs 1,200.

### Effect

The order is fixed.

1. **Splits,** for each `OnRoad` endpoint, `from` first. To split road `r` at `a = at_m`:
   - Append node `m` at the cut point, with control `Priority`. Use M10b's cut rule, so both pieces contain the cut vertex.
   - Re-point `r` to `[start..cut]`, and move its `to` endpoint to `m` with `set_endpoint`.
   - Append `r2 = append_like(r, m, old_to, [cut..end], layer(r))`.
2. **The road:** append the new road from the resolved `from` node to the resolved `to` node.

- **Changed ids:** the new road, both of its endpoint nodes, and for each split: `r`, `r2`, `m` and `old_to`.
- **Inverse:** `UndoAddRoad { road_len_before, node_len_before, splits: Vec<SplitRecord> }`, where `SplitRecord { road, r2, old_range, old_length, old_to, cut }`.

### Vehicles on apply

These run per split, before M10a's generic pass.

**Moves:**

| Where the vehicle is | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| `r`'s forward link, `s ≥ a` | `r2`'s forward link at `s − a` |
| `r`'s backward link, `s < len − a` | `r2`'s backward link at the same `s` |
| `r`'s backward link, otherwise | Stays at `s − (len − a)` |

**Route splice,** for every live vehicle, at every index `i ≥ cursor` holding a link of `r` (a route can hold both, after a U-turn at a dead end), each per its row, working from the highest index down:

| Link at `i` | Insert | New cursor |
|---|---|---|
| `r_fwd` | `r2_fwd` at `i + 1` | `i + 1` for a vehicle moved to `r2_fwd`, or in a movement at `old_to` from `r_fwd`. Otherwise unchanged |
| `r_bwd` | `r2_bwd` at `i` | `i` for a vehicle moved to `r2_bwd`. `i + 1` for every other vehicle with `i == cursor`: one that stays on `r_bwd`, or one in a movement from `r_bwd` at `r`'s `from` node. Otherwise unchanged |

- For a movement-placed vehicle, the links at `cursor` and `cursor + 1` are its movement's pair, and the same rules apply.
- The new routes are appended to the arena, as in M10b.
- Moved vehicles get M10b's junction-state reset: `committed`, `arrival_tick`, `wait_ticks` and `stopped_at_line`.

**Movements at `old_to`:**
- Before `old_to` is invalidated, record the `(from_link, to_link)` pairs of the vehicles in its movements.
- Substitute `r_fwd → r2_fwd` and `r_bwd → r2_bwd` in those pairs.
- The generic pass then remaps them to the new movements, so vehicles in that junction are not stranded.

The splice and the substitution keep every through route valid, so a split never triggers a wave of reroutes.

### `UndoAddRoad`, in this fixed order

0. Record `(from_link, to_link)` for vehicles in movements at the new road's endpoint nodes, and at both ends of every split road. In the pairs at each `old_to`, substitute `r2_fwd → r_fwd` and `r2_bwd → r_bwd`.
1. Strand every vehicle on a link of the new road, or in a movement from or to one.
2. For each split, in reverse order:
   1. Strand vehicles in movements at `m`.
   2. Move vehicles back:
      - `r2` forward at `s` → `r` forward at `s + a`.
      - `r2` backward at `s` → `r` backward at `s`.
      - `r` backward at `s` → `s + (len − a)`.
   3. Remove the `r2` entries from every live route, through the arena. After removing the entry at index `j`:
      - a cursor `> j` decrements
      - a cursor `== j` moves to `j − 1` when the removed entry was `r2_fwd`, which is now `r_fwd`
      - a cursor `== j` stays at `j` when it was `r2_bwd`, which is now `r_bwd`
   4. Restore `r`'s range, length and endpoint (`set_endpoint(r, m, old_to)`).
3. Set `deleted = true` on the new road and every `r2`, then `version += 1`. Invalidate the affected junctions, then call `ensure_graph`.
4. With no limit, reroute every live vehicle whose `route[cursor..]` references a link of the new road. A failed reroute strands it.
5. Truncate roads and nodes.
6. Run M10a's generic vehicle pass on the surviving ids, with the pairs recorded in step 0.

## Build-road tool (web)

### Toolbar (`hud/toolbar.ts`)

- A bottom-centre chip row with `Select` (key `S`, the default) and `Road` (key `R`).
- Only the challenge and sandbox screens show it.
- The active tool's chip has `aria-pressed="true"`.

### Road tool (`edit/tools/road-tool.ts`, `edit/tools/snap.ts`, `edit/tools/road-preview.ts`)

**Options row**, shown above the toolbar while the road tool is active:
- Lane presets: `1+1`, `2+2`, `3+3`, `1 →` and `2 →`. The default is `1+1`.
- `Elevation − n +`, with n from 0 to 2.
- A `Curve` toggle.

**`snapEndpoint(x, y, lookups)`**, pure and vitest-covered. `lookups` is a small interface over `pickNode`, `pickRoad` and the road store's points, so tests pass fakes:
1. `pickNode` within 12 px gives `Node`.
2. Otherwise, `pickRoad` within 8 px gives `OnRoad`. `at_m` is the projection onto the road's polyline, read from the road store.
   - When `at_m < 10` or `at_m > length − 10`, it snaps to that end's node instead.
3. Otherwise it gives `null`.

**Flow:**
- Clicks use `edit-controller`'s click-vs-drag rule (a pointerup within 4 px), so camera drags never place points.
- `R` and `S` respect `isEditableTarget`.

1. A click sets the start, which must snap.
2. With `Curve` on, the next click sets `via` at the raw pointer position.
3. The final click sets the end, which must snap.
4. Pointer moves update the preview.

**Preview:**
- The client computes the same geometry as Rust: straight, or the quadratic Bézier at 16 segments.
- The preview is drawn in `layers.selection`: white at alpha 0.6 with the road's width (lanes × 3.2 m) when the quote is `Ok`, and `#E5484D` at alpha 0.5 when it is `Err`.
- The command is quoted, debounced by 80 ms. The tooltip chip shows `$640 · New road` plus `Elevation: 1` when the layer is above 0, or the error message.
- When the end does not snap, the preview is a red line to the raw pointer with the message "Snap to a road or junction", and nothing is quoted.

**Commit:** the final click sends the command when the last quote was `Ok`. The tool then waits for a new start.

**Cancel:** `Esc` or a right-click cancels the current road. A second `Esc` returns to `Select`.

**Interaction with the select tool:** while the road tool is active, the select tool's hover, selection, Delete and inspector are off. `Ctrl+Z` still works.

`hud/messages.ts` gets messages for every new error.

## Tests

1. **`add_road_straight`:** on `grid_city(10×10 @ 150)`, add a 1+1 road from node (2,2) to node (3,3), the diagonal of one block.
   - The road and node counts grow by 1 and 0.
   - A trip from node (2,2) to node (3,3) routes over the new road.
   - With traffic at 3,000 vph for 2,000 ticks, `check_invariants` holds.
2. **`add_road_on_road`:** from node (2,2) to `OnRoad` at the middle of the road between nodes (3,2) and (3,3), with traffic running on that road.
   - The road and node counts grow by 2 and 1.
   - Vehicles on the split road move per the table, and their routes still pass `route_is_connected`, a test helper that checks `route[cursor..]` only, since a route's history before the cursor is never spliced.
   - `check_invariants` holds for 1,000 ticks.
   - `stranded == 0`, including vehicles inside the junction at `old_to`.
3. **`add_road_crossing`:** from node (2,2) to node (4,3):
   - At layer 0 it fails with `CrossesRoad`.
   - At layer 1 it succeeds, and vehicles use it.
4. **`add_road_undo`:** while traffic flows, add a road with two `OnRoad` ends: from the middle of the road between nodes (2,2) and (3,2), to the middle of the road between nodes (2,3) and (3,3). Step 100 ticks, `Undo`, then step 200 ticks.
   - `content_hash`, the counts and the geometry of both split roads equal the originals.
   - `check_invariants` holds every tick, and no live route has a link id ≥ `2 × road_count`.
   - The only stranded vehicles are those on the new road or in movements at an `m` node at undo time.
5. **`add_road_errors`:** each new `EditError`.
6. **`curved_geometry`:** a curved road has 17 points. Its first and last points equal the endpoint positions, and its midpoint equals the Bézier at `t = 0.5`.
7. **Determinism:** M10a's determinism test gains an `AddRoad` with a split, and two runs still match.
8. **vitest:**
   - `snap.test.ts`: node first, the road projection, and end snapping.
   - `road-preview.test.ts`: the TS geometry equals the Rust sample points for a fixed input. The points live in a committed JSON fixture, `web/src/edit/tools/curve-fixture.json`, written once. The Rust test reads it with `include_str!` and asserts equality, so `cargo test` never writes into the source tree.
   - `messages.test.ts` additions.
9. **Playwright** (`e2e/build-road.spec.ts`, `test.setTimeout(90_000)`), at `/?map=synthetic&vph=0&cx=375&cy=375&z=3`, the block between nodes (2,2) and (3,3). The screen is 1280 × 720, so node (2,2) is at screen (415, 135) and node (3,3) at (865, 585).
