# M6: junction rules

Goal: vehicles obey junction control. They stop at red, handle amber, yield by rank and time gap, stop at stop signs, take turns at all-way stops, keep out of full target lanes and full signal clusters, and time out of long yields. After M6, vehicles in conflicting movements no longer overlap.

This implements `docs/plan.md` §3.5 "Entry rule", "Signals" and "Priority rank". Every value below is fixed.

## Constants

```rust
pub const DECISION_MARGIN: f64 = 5.0;
pub const CONFLICT_CLEAR_MARGIN: f64 = 5.0;
pub const PRIORITY_GAP: f64 = 2.5;
pub const MINOR_GAP: f64 = 4.0;
pub const STOPPED_SPEED: f64 = 0.5;
pub const STOP_LINE_WINDOW: f64 = 3.0;
pub const WAIT_TIMEOUT_TICKS: u64 = 300;
```

## Vehicle state additions (`VehicleStore`)

- `committed: Vec<bool>`: set once the entry rule passes, and cleared when the vehicle enters the movement.
- `arrival_tick: Vec<u64>`: set when the vehicle first enters the decision zone of its current link, and reset to `u64::MAX` when it leaves the link.
- `stopped_at_line: Vec<bool>`: set once `v < STOPPED_SPEED` while `span_end − s ≤ STOP_LINE_WINDOW + IDM_MIN_GAP`, and cleared on leaving the link.
- All four are included in `state_hash`.

## Definitions

- **Decision zone:** a link-placed vehicle is in its decision zone when `span_end − s ≤ v²/(2·IDM_COMFORT_DECEL) + DECISION_MARGIN` and the route has a next link.
- **The vehicle's movement:** `m`, which is `(to_node, movement_index(link, route[cursor+1]))`.
- **Node control:** `Control` of the node. `Stop`, `AllWayStop` and `Yield` apply as in the plan. Signal state comes from `network.signal_state(link, tick)`, where `None` means unsignalized.
- **Minor approach:** at a `Stop` or `Yield` node, an approach whose incoming class rank is below the node's highest incoming class rank.
- **Signal priority of an approach `L`:**
  - 1 if `signal_state(L)` is `Green` or `Amber`, or if `L` is internal to a cluster.
  - Otherwise 0.
- **Effective rank of a movement:** `(signal_priority(from_link), rank.0, rank.1)`, compared lexicographically.
  - At an `AllWayStop` node, every movement has the same effective rank, so arrival tick alone decides.
- **Beats:** vehicle A beats vehicle B when A's effective rank is higher. On equal rank, the earlier `arrival_tick` wins, then the lower vehicle id.
  - A vehicle not yet in its decision zone has `arrival_tick = u64::MAX`.

## Entry rule (`src/vehicle/entry.rs`)

`pub fn may_enter(ctx: &RuleContext, slot: u32) -> bool` passes only when every check below passes. Each check is its own small function.

1. **`signal_allows`:**
   - No signal: pass.
   - `Green`: pass.
   - `Red`: fail.
   - `Amber`: pass only if the vehicle cannot stop in time (`span_end − s ≤ v²/(2·IDM_COMFORT_DECEL)`).
2. **`stop_satisfied`:**
   - At an `AllWayStop` node, or on a minor approach of a `Stop` node, the vehicle must have `stopped_at_line`.
   - Otherwise pass.
3. **`conflicts_clear`:** for each conflict `c` of `m`, with other movement `m2`, check that:
   - no vehicle in `m2` has `s < c.s_other + CONFLICT_CLEAR_MARGIN`, and
   - no committed vehicle is still on a link heading into `m2`.
4. **`no_priority_approach`:** for each conflict `c` of `m`, find the vehicles approaching `m2`.
   - An approaching vehicle is the front vehicle of each lane of `m2.from_link` whose next movement is `m2`.
   - Skip vehicles that are stopped by `signal_allows` or `stop_satisfied` for their own approach, unless they are committed.
   - For each remaining approaching vehicle B: `time = (dist_to_stop_line(B) + c.s_other) / max(v_B, 1.0)`.
   - The gap is `MINOR_GAP` if the deciding vehicle is on a minor approach, otherwise `PRIORITY_GAP`.
   - Fail if `time ≤ gap` and B beats the deciding vehicle.
   - Waived when `tick − arrival_tick ≥ WAIT_TIMEOUT_TICKS`.
5. **`has_space`:**
   - Let `target = route[cursor+1]` and `lane = landing lane`.
   - Pass when the last vehicle on `(target, lane)` has `s − span_start ≥ CAR_LENGTH + IDM_MIN_GAP`, or there is none.
   - **Cluster rule:** when the node belongs to a signal cluster and the current link is an approach (not internal), walk the route forward past every internal link. The first non-internal link must have at least one lane that passes the same test. Treat a route that ends inside the cluster as having space.

`RuleContext` holds `&Network`, `&VehicleStore`, `&Occupancy` and `tick`, which is 4 fields.

## Step changes (`Sim::step`)

After rebuilding the occupancy and before computing accelerations, add:

- **2a. Zone bookkeeping**, for every live link-placed vehicle in slot order:
  - Set `arrival_tick` when it enters the zone and it is `u64::MAX`.
  - Update `stopped_at_line`.
- **2b. Decisions:**
  1. For every vehicle in its decision zone that is not committed, compute `may_enter` into a scratch `Vec<bool>`. This pass is read-only, so the result does not depend on evaluation order.
  2. Then set `committed = true` for every vehicle whose decision passed.

In the acceleration pass, a link-placed vehicle in its decision zone that is not committed gets a stop-line obstacle:
- `gap = span_end − s`, with leader speed 0.
- It uses whichever of the stop line and the vehicle leader has the smaller gap.

Transitions:
- A link → movement transition only happens when the vehicle is committed.
- An uncommitted vehicle whose `s` would pass `span_end` is clamped to `span_end` with `v = 0`. The stop-line obstacle normally prevents this.
- Entering a movement clears `committed`, `arrival_tick` and `stopped_at_line`.

## Tests (`crates/sim-core/tests/`)

Add a helper to `tests/support/`: `run_until(sim, max_ticks, predicate)`. Add an invariant checker, `check_invariants(sim)`, that asserts:
- No NaN, and `v ≥ 0`.
- Same-lane gaps are ≥ 0 on links and within movements.
- For every crossing (non-merge) conflict, no two vehicles are each within 2.5 m of that conflict point in their respective movements at the same tick.

Tests:

1. **`signal_red_stop`:**
   - On `four_way(2, 200.0)`, spawn an east-approach vehicle (link 2) at tick 0, while phase 0 is north–south green.
   - It stops with `s ≤ span_end`, stays stopped until tick 350, then enters.
2. **`signal_amber`:**
   - Set up two north-approach vehicles at tick 290 (by spawning earlier): one within its stopping distance of the line when amber starts at tick 300, and one far from it.
   - The near one enters during amber. The far one stops.
3. **`permissive_left`:** a north-approach left-turner with opposing south-approach through traffic, spawned every 30 ticks for 400 ticks. The left-turner never occupies its crossing conflict while a through vehicle is within 2.5 m of it (via `check_invariants`), and it enters within the green phase or the next one.
4. **`t_junction_priority`:**
   - Major-road traffic on `t_junction()`, plus one minor vehicle.
   - Major vehicles never stop for the minor vehicle: their speed at the node stays at least 80 % of `v0`.
   - The minor vehicle enters only in a gap, or after the timeout.
5. **`stop_sign`:** `t_junction` with `Control::Stop` at node 0. The minor vehicle reaches `v < 0.5` before entering.
6. **`all_way_stop`:** `four_way` with `Control::AllWayStop` and one vehicle on each of 3 approaches, spawned 5 ticks apart. They enter in arrival order.
7. **`spillback`:** unit-test `has_space` with a hand-built `Sim` on `four_way(1, 200.0)`.
   - Spawn a vehicle A on the east exit link, then step until A is 3 m past that link's `span_start`. Freeze it by setting its route to end there.
   - A north-approach vehicle routed into that exit must not commit while A is within `CAR_LENGTH + IDM_MIN_GAP` of the span start.
   - It must commit once A moves on.
8. **`cluster_flow`:** on `dual_carriageway_cross()`, spawn random routes (through, left and right for each approach) every 20 ticks for 3,000 ticks.
   - `check_invariants` passes every tick.
   - Internal links never get a signal state.
   - Every vehicle despawns within 3,000 ticks of spawning.
   - No vehicle waits more than 3,000 ticks at any stop line.
9. **`timeout`:**
   - On `t_junction`, run a continuous major stream spaced 40 m apart, too tight for a 4 s gap.
   - The minor vehicle enters after `WAIT_TIMEOUT_TICKS`, in a moment when `conflicts_clear` holds, and `check_invariants` still passes.
10. **`low_demand_all_fixtures`:** on `four_way`, `t_junction`, `one_way_pair` and `dual_carriageway_cross`, spawn every 40 ticks with random valid 2- or 3-link routes for 6,000 ticks.
    - Invariants hold.
    - Every vehicle despawns within 3,000 ticks.
11. **`determinism_m5.rs`:** update `FOUR_WAY_HASH_5000` to the new value. The rules change the trajectories. Two runs must still match.
