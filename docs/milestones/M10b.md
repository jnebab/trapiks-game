# M10b: flyovers

Goal: the player can build a flyover at a junction. Two opposite roads are lifted over the junction, so through traffic between them bypasses it completely, and everything else still uses the junction.

This is the first edit that appends roads and nodes, so it also makes road geometry editable while keeping every id stable. Undo truncates the appended ids.

This implements plan §3.5 ("Id stability") and the `BuildFlyover` row of §3.6.

## Geometry becomes range-based (`network/road.rs`)

- `RoadStore.point_start: Vec<u32>` becomes `point_range: Vec<(u32, u32)>`, which is `(start, len)`.
- `points` and `cumulative` stay append-only.
- Re-pointing a road to new geometry appends the new points and updates its range. Old points become unreferenced garbage, which is accepted because edits are rare.
- `points(road)`, `cumulative(road)` and `length(road)` read through the range.
- Every existing caller keeps working, and the tests stay green.

## Appending (`network/append.rs`)

- `RoadStore::append(spec, points) -> u32` and `NodeStore::append(pos) -> u32` push to every column. The control is `Priority` and the incident list is empty.
- `Network::append_*` keeps these structures in sync:
  - the node incident lists, which stay sorted
  - the `junctions` cache length
  - the `SpatialGrid`, fully rebuilt when a structural version counter changes, at the end of the edit
- `truncate_roads(len)` and `truncate_nodes(len)` undo appends.
  - They also remove the truncated ids from every incident list.
  - They are only valid for the most recently appended ids. The undo stack is LIFO, so this always holds.

## `EditCommand::BuildFlyover { node: u32, through: [u32; 2] }`

### Validation (new `EditError` variants)

- The node exists and has an active degree ≥ 3 (`NotAJunction`).
- `through[0] != through[1]`, and both are active roads incident to the node (`RoadNotIncident`).
- **Straightness:** with `d0` and `d1` the departing directions of the two roads at the node, both at 10 m, the angle between them is at least 150° (`FlyoverNotStraight`).
- **Length:** each road has length ≥ 30 m (`FlyoverTooShort`).
- Region mode: both roads must be active in the region (`OutsideRegion`).

### Effect, for each through road `r`

1. `d = min(80, length(r) / 2)`, measured from the node along `r`.
2. Append a node `m_r` at the point at distance `d` from the node.
3. Re-point `r` to its outer part: its geometry runs from the far end to `m_r`, and its endpoint at the node becomes `m_r`.
4. Append the inner road `r'`, with `r`'s class, lanes, speed and name, `layer = layer(r) + 1`, and the inner geometry. Its orientation mirrors `r`:
   - If `r` arrived at the node (`r.to == node`), `r'` runs `m_r → n'`.
   - Otherwise `r'` runs `n' → m_r`.

The flyover node `n'` is appended once, at the node's position, before the inner roads.

Afterwards:
- The original node keeps its other roads. Its degree drops by 2, and a degree-2 or smaller node loses any signal cluster.
- Bans at the node that mention `r` become inert, because `r` no longer touches the node. They are kept, so undo restores the exact set.

### Cost and inverse

- **Cost:** `2000 + round(10 × (len(r_a') + len(r_b')))`.
- **Inverse:** `UndoFlyover { node, roads: [r_a, r_b], old_ranges, old_ends, road_len_before, node_len_before }`. It restores both roads' ranges and endpoints, then truncates roads and nodes to their previous lengths.
- **Changed ids:** the result reports the node, `n'`, both `m` nodes, both original roads and both inner roads.

### Afterwards

- Invalidate the junctions at the node, `n'`, both `m` nodes and both far ends.
- Signals rebuild, `version += 1`, and the route graph, demand tables and `LinkCosts` resize through the version check.
- Landmark tables keep their size; new link ids read as missing, which M7a already handles.
- **Vehicles:**
  - Vehicles on a re-pointed road keep their `s` only when `s` is within the outer part's span. Otherwise they are moved onto the inner road at `s − outer length`, with the same lane.
  - Vehicles in a movement at the node whose movement no longer exists are stranded, as in M10a's remap.
  - Routes through the old node that used the lifted roads are flagged for reroute.

## Rendering note for M11

`networkDelta` must carry appended roads and nodes, plus re-pointed geometry. M11 handles this. M10b only reports the changed ids.

## Tests

1. **`flyover_bypasses_junction`:** on `four_way(2, 200.0)`, `BuildFlyover { node: 0, through: [0, 2] }` (north–south).
   - Road and node counts grow by 2 and 3.
   - Node 0 now has 2 active roads.
   - A vehicle north → south routes through `n'`, and its route never contains a movement at node 0.
   - A vehicle east → west routes through node 0.
   - With both streams running for 2,000 ticks, `check_invariants` holds, and no north–south vehicle ever waits at node 0.
2. **`flyover_undo`:** apply, step 100 ticks, `Undo`, step.
   - `content_hash`, road count and node count equal the originals.
   - Road 0's geometry equals the original point for point.
3. **`flyover_errors`:** each new `EditError`, plus `NotAJunction` on a degree-2 node.
4. **`flyover_then_edit_undo_twice`:** flyover, then `SetLanes` on an inner road, then `Undo` twice restores everything.
5. **`flyover_region`:** in region mode, a flyover on an inactive road is rejected.
6. **Determinism:** the M10a determinism test gains a flyover in its edit list, and two runs still match.
